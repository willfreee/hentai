Finding them never cured the craving. When a scene hit every note, you did not close the browser in permanent peace. You kept the tab open, looked for more tabs to see if anything better existed, and prolonged the session.

The primitive brain treats every discovery as a reason to search further. A dopamine-driven system cannot register permanent completion; its biological job is to keep hunting for novelty. The perfect scene is a moving horizon, promising rest while forcing you to keep scrolling.

The fear of missing out operates on the exact same trick. Users obsessively check artist feeds on Pixiv or Twitter, terrified that quitting will cause them to miss an unprecedented masterpiece.
